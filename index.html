What you get

A calmer clinic in four ways

Never miss a patient

Anastasia answers calls and WhatsApp messages 24/7 in Greek and English, including while you are examining a patient, at lunch or after hours.

Urgent patients first

Every request is triaged as Urgent, Soon or Routine/GeSY. Urgent buffer slots are protected, and red-flag symptoms go straight to 112 or to you. If anything is unclear, the case is flagged for a call-back.

Prepared, and still in control

Each booking lands on a colour-coded Anastasia calendar with a 3-bullet case note. Anything sensitive waits for your one-letter A/B/C/D reply on WhatsApp.

Supplier & rep calls handled too

Anastasia also takes clear messages from suppliers, medical reps, labs, insurers and other doctors, labelled separately so they never get mixed up with patient cases, and never shares your schedule.

Getting started

Your clinic in 3 steps

Patients keep calling the number they know, and your staff need no new app.

  1. 1

    Forward your existing number

    Set your clinic landline or mobile to forward calls to Anastasia when the line is busy or not answered. Patients keep calling the number they already know.

    Landlines work too. Conditional call forwarding (busy / no answer) is a standard feature on most landline and mobile plans.

  2. 2

    Set up your clinic WhatsApp

    Anastasia answers on the official WhatsApp Business Platform, either on a dedicated MyDocDesk line for your clinic or on your existing business number moved to the platform. She answers messages and voice notes, sends confirmations and collects photos or lab results before the visit.

  3. 3

    Choose your rules

    Set your booking hours, which slots are kept as urgent buffers, and what needs your A/B/C/D approval. Change them at any time.
