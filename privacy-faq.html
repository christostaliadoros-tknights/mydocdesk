Privacy & trust

Built for patient data from day one

How Anastasia handles your patients' information and where the doctor stays in control.

GDPR and health data

We follow GDPR and treat health information as special-category data. Only the details needed to book and brief the doctor are collected, and only for that purpose.

Clear roles, DPA included

Your clinic is the data controller and MyDocDesk (T-Knights Consulting Ltd) is the data processor. A data processing agreement (DPA) is signed with every clinic before go-live, and the full list of subprocessors is provided with it, or earlier on request.

Short retention, the doctor keeps the record

Case notes are delivered to your clinic's own calendar and WhatsApp, so the medical record stays with the doctor. Call audio recording is off by default. Transcripts and call logs are deleted automatically after 30 days. If you cancel, any remaining clinic data is returned or deleted within 30 days.

Audit trail of every action

Every booking, reply, escalation and approval is logged with time and source, so the clinic can see who did what and when. Bookings and approvals also stay in the clinic's own calendar and WhatsApp.

The doctor has the final say

Urgent buffers, GeSY renewals and anything unusual wait for the doctor's A/B/C/D reply. Anastasia suggests; the doctor decides.

Anastasia never diagnoses

She asks standard intake and red-flag questions, then routes the patient. Red-flag symptoms are escalated to 112 or the doctor immediately.

What callers hear first (consent notice)

Illustrative example – not a real clinic or patient

“Hello, you've reached Dr. Nicolaou's clinic. I'm Anastasia, the clinic's AI assistant. This call is handled by an AI and a transcript is kept to prepare your appointment, in line with the clinic's privacy policy. If you'd rather speak to a person, just say so and the clinic will call you back.”

The wording is set by each clinic and can be adjusted with its data-protection adviser.

Safety & limitations

What Anastasia does, and what she never does

A receptionist, not a clinician. Here is exactly where her role ends.

Not a medical device

Anastasia is a reception assistant, not a medical device. She does not diagnose, give medical advice or replace clinical judgement.

A conservative red-flag script

She follows a cautious red-flag script that always errs on the side of safety: when in doubt, the caller is told to call 112 or the case is marked for an urgent callback.

If she's unsure or the call drops

If Anastasia is unsure, the line is unclear or the call is cut off, the case is flagged for the clinic to call back and the doctor is notified.

The doctor makes every clinical decision

Anastasia collects information, books within your rules and passes urgent cases on. All clinical decisions stay with the doctor.

Your clinic approves the script

During onboarding, your clinic reviews and approves the triage questions and red-flag list before Anastasia speaks to any patient.

FAQ

Questions doctors ask us

Do I need a new phone number?

No. Patients keep calling your existing clinic number. It forwards to Anastasia only when the line is busy or not answered, so nothing changes for them.

Does it work with my landline?

Yes. Conditional call forwarding (busy / no answer) is a standard feature on most landline and mobile plans, including Cyta, Epic and Cablenet (*67* / *61*). Landline callers who don't use WhatsApp get an SMS confirmation.

Will my staff still answer calls?

Yes. When you or your staff pick up, nothing changes. Anastasia only takes the calls that would otherwise be missed: busy lines, no answer, lunch breaks and after hours.

How does WhatsApp work?

MyDocDesk runs on the official WhatsApp Business Platform. You can choose a dedicated MyDocDesk WhatsApp line for your clinic (the fastest option), or we migrate your existing business number to the platform. Once migrated, that number no longer works in the regular WhatsApp app. Either way, patients still call your usual clinic number, and Anastasia answers text and voice notes, sends booking confirmations and collects photos or lab results before the visit.

Does Anastasia speak Greek?

Yes. She speaks Greek and English and answers in the language the patient uses. You can hear both in the live demo.

How does it handle GeSY patients?

Anastasia records the patient's GeSY details during intake. Prescription renewals and referrals are grouped into one end-of-day list for you to approve, instead of taking exam slots.

What if a patient has an emergency?

Anastasia asks standard red-flag questions. If there are red-flag symptoms she does not book an appointment: she tells the caller to call 112 and alerts you straight away. If she is unsure or the call drops, the case is flagged for the clinic to call back. She never diagnoses.

Who decides what gets booked?

You do. You set the booking hours, the urgent buffer slots and what needs your approval. Anything that touches an urgent buffer waits for your one-letter A/B/C/D reply.

How is patient data protected?

We follow GDPR and treat health information as special-category data. Only the details needed to book and brief you are collected. Your clinic is the data controller and MyDocDesk (T-Knights Consulting Ltd) is the data processor; a data processing agreement (DPA) is signed before go-live. Call audio recording is off by default, transcripts and call logs are deleted automatically after 30 days, and the medical record stays with you in your own calendar and WhatsApp. See the privacy section above.

What happens if we use more than 300 minutes?

Your plan includes 300 AI call minutes per month. You can add a prepaid top-up pack of 200 extra minutes for €29. If the minutes run out and you don't buy a top-up, Anastasia keeps handling WhatsApp and calls simply ring through to your clinic's own line as normal. No surprise bills, no overage invoices.

How do I cancel?

Give one month's notice. There is no long contract. We switch Anastasia off, your call forwarding goes back to normal and patients keep calling the same number.
