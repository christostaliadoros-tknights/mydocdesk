Privacy & trust

Built for patient data from day one

How Anastasia handles your patients' information and where the doctor stays in control.

🇪🇺

GDPR by design

Built around GDPR principles: only the details needed to book and brief the doctor are collected, for that purpose only. Your clinic stays the data controller.

🗄️

Designed for EU hosting

The platform is designed to keep call transcripts, case notes and messages on servers located in the EU.

⏳

Retention controls

The clinic decides how long transcripts and recordings are kept, and they are deleted automatically afterwards. Patients can ask the clinic for their data to be removed.

🧾

Audit trail of every action

Every booking, reply, escalation and approval is logged with time and source, so the clinic can always see who did what and when.

👩‍⚕️

The doctor has the final say

Urgent buffers, GeSY renewals and anything unusual wait for the doctor's A/B/C/D reply. Anastasia suggests; the doctor decides.

⚕️

Anastasia never diagnoses

She asks standard intake and red-flag questions, then routes the patient. Red-flag symptoms are escalated to 112 or the doctor immediately.

What callers hear first (consent notice)
“Hello, you've reached Dr. Nicolaou's clinic. I'm Anastasia, the clinic's AI assistant. This call is handled by an AI and a transcript is kept to prepare your appointment, in line with the clinic's privacy policy. If you'd rather speak to a person, just say so and the clinic will call you back.”

The wording is set by each clinic and can be adjusted with its data-protection adviser.

FAQ

Questions doctors ask us

Do I need a new phone number?

No. Patients keep calling your existing clinic number. It forwards to Anastasia only when the line is busy or not answered, so nothing changes for them.

Does it work with my landline?

Yes. Conditional call forwarding (busy / no answer) is a standard feature on most landline and mobile plans, including Cyta, Epic and Cablenet (*67* / *61*). Landline callers who don't use WhatsApp get an SMS confirmation.

Will my staff still answer calls?

Yes. When you or your staff pick up, nothing changes. Anastasia only takes the calls that would otherwise be missed: busy lines, no answer, lunch breaks and after hours.

How does WhatsApp work?

Anastasia answers the clinic's WhatsApp: text and voice notes, booking confirmations, and photos or lab results sent before the visit. We go through the best way to connect your current WhatsApp number on the pilot call.

Does Anastasia speak Greek?

Yes. She speaks Greek and English and answers in the language the patient uses. You can hear both in the live demo.

How does it handle GeSY patients?

Anastasia records the patient's GeSY details during intake. Prescription renewals and referrals are grouped into one end-of-day list for you to approve, instead of taking exam slots.

What if a patient has an emergency?

Anastasia asks standard red-flag questions. If there are red-flag symptoms she does not book an appointment: she tells the caller to call 112 and alerts you straight away. She never diagnoses.

Who decides what gets booked?

You do. You set the booking hours, the urgent buffer slots and what needs your approval. Anything that touches an urgent buffer waits for your one-letter A/B/C/D reply.

How is patient data protected?

MyDocDesk is built around GDPR principles: only the details needed to book and brief you are collected, your clinic stays the data controller, the platform is designed for EU hosting, and you decide how long transcripts are kept. See the privacy section above.

What happens if we use more than 300 minutes?

Your plan includes 300 AI call minutes per month. You can add a prepaid top-up pack of 200 extra minutes for €29. If the minutes run out and you don't buy a top-up, Anastasia keeps handling WhatsApp and calls simply ring through to your clinic's own line as normal. No surprise bills, no overage invoices.

How do I cancel?

Give one month's notice. There is no long contract. We switch Anastasia off, your call forwarding goes back to normal and patients keep calling the same number.
