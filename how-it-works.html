How it works

From the first ring to your one-letter reply

Anastasia handles the front-desk work in six steps. Nothing changes for your patients, and nothing sensitive happens without you.

The flow at a glance

  1. 1 Patient calls Forwarded when busy or unanswered
  2. 2 …or sends a WhatsApp Text, voice notes, photos, lab PDFs
  3. 3 Triage P1 / P2 / P3 Urgent symptoms → advised to call 112
  4. 4 Case note 3 bullets before the visit
  5. 5 Anastasia calendar layer Colour-coded, never double-books
  6. 6 A / B / C / D approval You reply with one letter
P1 Urgent: same day P2 Soon: 24–48 h P3 Routine / GeSY: end-of-day list

Anastasia never diagnoses. If a caller describes urgent symptoms, she tells them to call 112 and flags the message to the doctor. She is not an emergency service and may not recognise every emergency. If she is unsure or the call drops, the case is flagged for a call-back. Safety & limitations

1

Calls are answered, even when you can't

Your clinic landline or mobile forwards to Anastasia only when the line is busy or not answered. Patients keep calling the number they already know.

She speaks Greek and English, tells callers they are speaking to the clinic's AI assistant, and asks standard intake questions: who the patient is, what the problem is, how long it has lasted and their GeSY details.

2

WhatsApp messages and voice notes too

Patients can message the clinic's WhatsApp. Anastasia answers text and voice notes, sends booking confirmations and asks for a photo or lab result when it helps the doctor prepare.

Landline callers who don't use WhatsApp get a standard SMS confirmation instead.

3

Every request is triaged by urgency

P1 Urgent Same-day problems, such as a child with high fever and ear pain or an acute toothache, are offered a protected urgent buffer slot, with your approval.

P2 Soon Rashes, lab reviews or dental treatment are booked in the next 24–48 hours, in a slot of the right length, with a photo or PDF collected first if useful.

P3 Routine / GeSY Repeat prescriptions and referrals go into one end-of-day list instead of taking an exam slot.

Callers who describe urgent symptoms are not booked in. Anastasia tells them to call 112 and flags the message to you straight away. She may not recognise every emergency and does not replace 112 or clinical assessment. If Anastasia is unsure, the line is unclear or the call drops, the case is flagged for the clinic to call back and you are notified.

4

A 3-bullet case note before the patient arrives

Each booking carries a short pre-visit note: the chief complaint, the GeSY ID and any photos or files the patient sent. You walk in already knowing why they came.

5

Booked on a colour-coded Anastasia calendar layer

Anastasia reads the busy times across your connected Google, Apple or Outlook calendars (personal, hospital rounds and the clinic calendar) so she never double-books, without exposing private details.

Her bookings go onto a separate, colour-coded "Anastasia AI Reception" calendar, so you can see at a glance what she booked.

6

You approve with one letter on WhatsApp

Urgent buffers, GeSY renewals and anything unusual wait for you. Anastasia sends a short WhatsApp/SMS message and you reply from your lock screen:

  • AApprove & send
  • BNext morning slot
  • CRequest a lab result or photo
  • DDoctor calls back

You also get a short morning digest and one end-of-day GeSY list to approve in a couple of minutes. Anastasia suggests; the doctor decides.
