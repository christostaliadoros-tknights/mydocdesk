Zero-disruption setup

You keep your existing clinic number. Your landline or mobile forwards to Anastasia only when the line is busy or not answered, so when you or your staff pick up, nothing changes.

Works with Cyta, Epic or Cablenet lines via the standard forwarding codes *67* (busy) and *61* (no answer). We set it up with you.

🗓️

Cancel with one month's notice

No long contract. If MyDocDesk isn't right for your clinic, give one month's notice and we switch it off. Your call forwarding goes back to normal and patients keep calling the same number.
